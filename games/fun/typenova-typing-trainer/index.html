💡Tablet typing tips

Accuracy firstAim for 95%+ accuracy. Speed comes automatically once your fingers stop making mistakes.
Use both handsCyan-edged keys are your left hand, pink-edged keys your right. Split the work at T/G/B and Y/H/N.
Turn off autocorrectIn Gboard settings → Text correction, switch off auto-correction and suggestions so the trainer sees your real keystrokes.
Numbers on your keyboardLong-press Q–P to get 1–0. The guide shows "HOLD" on that key.
Tab = restartPress your Tab key (top-left) anytime to restart the current test instantly.
10 minutes a dayShort daily practice beats one long session. Keep your day streak alive!
